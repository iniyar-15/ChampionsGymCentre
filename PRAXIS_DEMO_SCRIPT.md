# Praxis — Product Demo Script (≈6 minutes)

A scene-by-scene script you can read while screen-recording (Loom, OBS, Windows
Game Bar, etc.). Each scene lists what to click/show and exactly what to say.
Tone: confident, conversational, benefits-first — talk to the pain point
before the feature.

> Praxis works for any skill-based institution — gymnastics, dance, swimming,
> martial arts, music, tuition centres, etc. During the demo, swap "students"
> / "athletes" for whatever fits your audience (e.g. "members", "learners").

---

## Scene 1 — Hook (0:00–0:20)

**Show:** Praxis login screen (staff or student — whichever looks cleanest)

**Say:**
"If you're running a sports academy, dance studio, or coaching centre, you
already know the drill — attendance on WhatsApp, fees tracked in a notebook
or Excel, and competition updates sent one by one to parents. It works...
until it doesn't. Today I want to show you Praxis — a complete management
platform built to run your entire academy from one place."

---

## Scene 2 — Brand & Login (0:20–0:35)

**Show:** Zoom on the Praxis logo + wordmark on the login screen, then log in
as an admin.

**Say:**
"Praxis gives every academy its own branded portal — for admins, coaches,
and for students and parents. Let's log in as an admin and take a look."

---

## Scene 3 — Admin Dashboard (0:35–1:05)

**Show:** Admin Dashboard — point at the overview cards (students, attendance,
fee collection, upcoming competitions, etc.)

**Say:**
"This is the admin dashboard — a single screen that tells you exactly what's
happening across the academy today: how many students are enrolled, how
attendance is trending, how much fee has come in this month, and what's
coming up. No more digging through spreadsheets to answer 'how are we doing?'"

---

## Scene 4 — Student Management & Onboarding (1:05–1:50)

**Show:** Students page → click "Add Student" → fill in a sample student
(name, parent name, phone, email, DOB, level, fee structure, batch) → Save.

**Say:**
"Adding a new student takes under a minute. You capture their contact
details, assign them to a skill level, a fee plan, and one or more batches —
all in one form."

**Show:** (cut/zoom to) mention the automatic welcome email.

**Say:**
"The moment you hit save, Praxis automatically creates their portal login and
sends a personalized welcome email — with their login details, the program
they've joined, and a motivational note to kick off their journey. No manual
follow-up needed."

**Show:** Edit the same student to show batches/levels persist correctly.

**Say:**
"And everything you enter — batches, levels, fee plans — is fully editable
later, so your records always stay accurate as students progress."

---

## Scene 5 — Attendance (1:50–2:20)

**Show:** Attendance page — select a batch and date, mark a few students
present/absent, save.

**Say:**
"Coaches can mark attendance for their batch in seconds, right from their
phone or laptop. That data rolls straight up into the dashboard and into
monthly reports — so you always know who's showing up, and who needs a
nudge."

---

## Scene 6 — Fee Collection (2:20–3:30)

**Show:** Fee Collection page (admin) — open a student's fee record.

**Say:**
"Now, the part every academy owner cares about most — fees. Praxis tracks
what's due, what's been paid, and what's outstanding, for every student,
every month."

**Show:** Click "Pay" → choose payment mode.

**Say:**
"When recording a payment, the admin picks how it was paid. If it's cash,
we simply ask — who received it? Pick from your list of coaches and admins,
so there's always accountability for cash collected on the ground."

**Show:** Switch payment mode to "Online" / "UPI" — show the breakdown
(base fee + transaction fee % + 18% GST on that fee = total).

**Say:**
"For digital payments, Praxis integrates directly with Razorpay. We
automatically add the payment gateway's transaction fee — plus GST on that
fee — so your academy never absorbs the cost of going digital. The
student sees one clear total and pays securely, right from their phone."

**Show:** After a payment is recorded, show the auto-generated PDF receipt
(open or download it) and mention the email.

**Say:**
"As soon as a payment is recorded — by the admin or by the student — Praxis
generates a professional, GST-compliant fee receipt and emails it
automatically. Every receipt is also stored, so it can be reprinted or
re-sent anytime, for audits or parent requests."

---

## Scene 7 — Automated Billing & Reminders (3:30–4:00)

**Show:** Reports / fee dashboard, or just narrate over the fees page.

**Say:**
"You don't even need to raise monthly bills manually. On the 1st of every
month, Praxis automatically generates each student's fee — prorated if they
joined mid-month. Then on the 3rd, 7th, and 10th, anyone who hasn't paid
gets an automatic reminder email, with the tone getting a little more urgent
as the due date approaches. It's like having a billing assistant that never
forgets."

---

## Scene 8 — Competition Management (4:00–5:10)

**Show:** Competitions page → open or create a competition → add an age
group with level, entry fee, date/time, and apparatus/events.

**Say:**
"Competitions are a huge part of any academy — and also a huge admin
headache. In Praxis, you create a competition, then define age groups with
their own eligibility rules: age range, skill level, entry fee, date, time,
and venue."

**Show:** Scroll to "Eligible students" list under an age group, then click
"Shortlist" for one or two students.

**Say:**
"Praxis automatically matches your students against those rules and shows
you exactly who's eligible. Shortlist the ones you're sending — and the
moment you save, Praxis sends them a text message, an email, and an in-app
notification, all with the competition name, date, and venue. Zero manual
messaging."

**Show:** Switch to the student/parent portal → Competitions page → show
the shortlist notification and "Confirm Participation" button → click it,
check the entry-fee-paid box, select payment mode, confirm.

**Say:**
"On the student side, parents see the notification immediately, and confirm
participation with one tap — including whether the entry fee has been paid.
That status flows straight back to the admin, so you always know exactly who's
confirmed for the big day."

---

## Scene 9 — Student / Parent Portal (5:10–5:40)

**Show:** Tour the student portal — Profile, Fees, Attendance, Competitions.

**Say:**
"And this is the experience parents and students get — their own portal to
check class schedules, attendance history, fee status, pay online, and stay
on top of competitions. Everything an academy used to handle over phone
calls and WhatsApp groups, now self-service."

---

## Scene 10 — Reports (5:40–5:55)

**Show:** Admin Reports page — generate an attendance or fees report PDF.

**Say:**
"And whenever you need a bird's-eye view — for yourself, an investor, or a
franchise partner — Praxis generates clean PDF reports on attendance, fee
collection, and new enrollments with one click."

---

## Scene 11 — Closing / CTA (5:55–6:10)

**Show:** Back to dashboard or the Praxis logo screen.

**Say:**
"That's Praxis — one platform to manage students, attendance, fees,
payments, and competitions, with automated communication built in from day
one. If you're spending hours every week on admin instead of coaching, let's
talk about getting Praxis set up for your academy."

---

## Recording Tips

- Record in 1080p, landscape. Zoom browser to ~110% so text is readable.
- Use a test/demo account with realistic-looking sample data (not real
  student PII) before recording.
- Keep mouse movements slow and deliberate — pause briefly after each click
  before talking.
- Record each scene as a separate clip; it's much easier to re-take one
  20-second scene than the whole 6 minutes.
- Add captions/subtitles in your video editor — many prospects watch on mute.
